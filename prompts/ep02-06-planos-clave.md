episodio: los que hay que generar primero, porque si estos no salen, no hay serie.

# EPISODIO 2 — "EL CAMIÓN"

**2.K1 — La cadena humana de la carga** · 8 s
```
[SHOT] Medium shot, 35mm lens, handheld, slight pan following the load.
[SUBJECT] <RUSO> and two other men passing a 50 kg sack of rice hand to hand.
[ACTION] A sack is passed from the left hand of one man to the right of the next in one
continuous heave; the man receiving it staggers half a step under the weight.
[SETTING] Back storeroom of a small Argentine grocery at night, metal shelving, stacked sacks,
one bare bulb, a half-lowered metal roller shutter at the far end.
[LIGHT] Single bare hanging bulb overhead, hard top light, everything else black.
[LOOK] <LOOK-1> night variant: warm tungsten key, deep black shadows.
[AUDIO] Grunting, sacks thudding, the roller shutter rattling, a distant car horn.
```

# EPISODIO 3 — "RUTA 3"

```
[SHOT] High aerial static shot, 50mm equivalent, no orbit, no movement.
[SUBJECT] A single perfectly straight two-lane highway across absolutely flat farmland at

[ACTION] The line of lights does not move at all; two headlights cross the field of view on

[SETTING] National Route 3, Buenos Aires province, night, no towns, no other light source in
any direction.
[LIGHT] Only vehicle lights; the landscape is black; a faint band of stars.
[LOOK] <LOOK-3> Cinematic 2.39:1, night, deep blue-black, red and white light points, grain.
[AUDIO] A low, continuous mass of idling engines and scattered horns, heard from far above.
NEGATIVE: + no drone orbit, no camera movement, no city glow, no modern car designs.
```
