# Los 14 prompts del primer video — listos para copiar y pegar

**No hay nada que completar.** Las fichas de personaje ya están pegadas adentro de cada prompt.
Se copia el bloque entero, se pega en el generador, y se piden 4 variantes.

Nombre de archivo de cada clip: `T01.mp4`, `T02.mp4` ... `T14.mp4`.
El script `03-ensamblar.sh` busca exactamente esos nombres.

# BLOQUE A — EL MUNDO NORMAL

## T01 · Campo de trigo al amanecer · 8 s

```
Wide static shot, 40mm lens, tripod, absolutely no camera movement.

An endless field of ripe golden wheat, no people, no buildings. The wheat moves slowly in a
light wind, in long waves that travel across the entire frame from left to right.

Setting: flat farmland outside Tres Arroyos, Buenos Aires province, Argentina, summer sunrise.
The horizon is dead flat and completely empty except for a single distant line of eucalyptus
trees far away on the left. No mountains, no hills.

Light: low golden sunrise sun from frame left, long soft shadows across the crop, clean pale
blue cloudless sky.

Look: cinematic 2.39:1 anamorphic framing, 35mm film grain, warm naturalistic palette of wheat
gold and pale blue, gentle contrast, deep focus f/8, everything sharp.

Audio: loud cicadas, a distant rooster, a far-off tractor engine. No music.

NEGATIVE: no text, no subtitles, no watermark, no logos, no people, no animals, no buildings,
no roads, no mountains, no hills, no clouds, no lens flare, no slow motion, no drone movement,
no camera movement, no CGI look.
```

## T02 · Los pájaros se van todos juntos · 6 s

```
Wide static low-angle shot, 28mm lens, tripod, no camera movement.

About forty small brown birds sitting close together on a rusted barbed wire fence in the
foreground. All the birds take off at exactly the same instant, in one single motion, and fly
out of the top right of frame, leaving the wire empty and vibrating.

Setting: the same flat wheat field at sunrise, weathered wooden fence posts and three strands
of rusted barbed wire filling the lower third of frame, golden wheat behind.

Light: backlit sunrise from behind the fence, the birds read as dark silhouettes against a
pale sky.

Look: cinematic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, warm gold and pale blue palette, deep focus f/8.

Audio: cicadas at full volume, then a burst of forty pairs of wings, and then complete silence
for the last three seconds. No music.

NEGATIVE: no text, no watermark, no people, no slow motion, no camera movement, no CGI birds,
no flocking swirl, no murmuration pattern, no lens flare.
```

## T03 · La perra mira hacia el este · 8 s

```
Low medium shot at dog height, 50mm lens, static tripod, no camera movement.

PAMPA — a medium-sized Argentine mixed-breed dog, German shepherd cross, 6 years old, short
light brown coat with a black mask on the muzzle and ears, the left ear folding forward, lean
with ribs faintly visible, an old brown leather collar with a small metal tag.

She sits on a cracked concrete sidewalk facing directly away from camera, down the length of
an empty street, ears rigid and upright, completely motionless. She growls low without moving
a muscle. She does not look back.

Setting: an empty small-town street in Tres Arroyos, Argentina, at dawn. Low brick and
plastered houses with barred windows, one parked 1990s pickup truck, concrete telephone poles
with sagging cables, a closed metal roller shutter on a workshop at frame right. The street
runs straight away from camera to a flat empty horizon.

Light: flat cool dawn light, the sun not yet risen, no shadows, pale gray-blue sky at the end
of the street.

Look: cinematic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, muted palette of brick, concrete and pale blue,
shallow focus f/2.8 on the dog with the street softening behind her.

Audio: total silence, then one low growl, then a metal roller shutter beginning to rise far
behind camera. No music.

NEGATIVE: no text, no watermark, no people, no other animals, no cars moving, no dog looking
at camera, no tail wagging, no cute expression, no camera movement, no slow motion.
```

## T04 · El mapa en la televisión · 6 s

```
Extreme close-up of an old CRT television screen, 85mm lens, static, slight moiré and
scanlines visible.

On the screen: a crude 1990s broadcast-news graphic. A simple map of the southern hemisphere
with two dotted white trajectory lines curving in from the top of frame, each ending in a red
dot — one red dot in the Pacific Ocean west of Chile, one red dot in the Atlantic Ocean east of
Buenos Aires. A red banner strip along the bottom of the graphic.

The graphic holds completely still, then the two red dots pulse once, together.

Setting: a dusty convex CRT television screen filling the whole frame, the reflection of a
kitchen window and a floral curtain faintly visible in the curved glass.

Light: only the television's own glow, greenish and unstable.

Look: cinematic 2.39:1, heavy video scanlines, chromatic fringing at the edges of the graphics,
35mm film grain over the top, low contrast.

Audio: distorted small TV speaker. A male Argentine news anchor, formal and flat: "Las
autoridades solicitan a la población mantener la calma. No hay motivo para el pánico." Then a
two-tone emergency alert beep.

NEGATIVE: no modern flat screen, no HD graphics, no English text, no logos, no news channel
branding, no anchor visible, no camera movement, no zoom.
```

## T05 · Camila entiende · 8 s

```
Close-up, 85mm lens, static framing, handheld with a very slight weight.

CAMILA — 34-year-old Argentine woman, 1.68 m, slim build. Fair skin with freckles across her
cheekbones. Dark brown wavy hair to the shoulders, wet from a shower, tied back loosely.
Green eyes, thick eyebrows, intense direct gaze. Thin gold metal-framed glasses. Gray
sweatshirt.

She is sitting down slowly onto a kitchen chair without looking at it, her eyes fixed on
something off-frame to the left. Her mouth opens very slightly. She does not blink for the
whole shot. One hand is still holding a set of car keys, forgotten, halfway raised.

Setting: a small 1970s Argentine kitchen, morning. Out of focus in the foreground, the
shoulder of an older woman in a pink quilted housecoat sitting at a table with floral oilcloth.
Behind, a refrigerator and a hanging cupboard.

Light: soft morning window light from frame left, warm, the right side of her face falling into
shadow, no fill light.

Look: cinematic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, warm naturalistic palette, very shallow focus f/2.0,
only her eyes sharp.

Audio: the television drops to a muffled underwater hum. Her breathing, shallow. A clock. No
music. No dialogue.

NEGATIVE: no text, no watermark, no crying, no tears, no dramatic gasp, no hand over mouth,
no smiling, no makeup, no camera movement, no zoom, no slow motion.
```

## T06 · La bandeja de agua · 8 s

```
Close-up, side angle at counter height, 50mm lens, static tripod.

A rectangular glass laboratory tray, half full of water, resting on a school laboratory
counter. Three small wooden blocks stand upright in the shallow end of the tray, like tiny
buildings.

A woman's hand enters frame from the right, lifts the far edge of the tray about fifteen
centimeters, and lets it drop. The water surges the full length of the tray, jumps the near
rim and spills over the counter and off the edge, knocking all three wooden blocks flat.

Setting: a dark, empty science classroom in an Argentine technical high school, lights off.
Brass gas taps and a deep sink out of focus behind. Dust on the counter.

Light: hard window light raking low across the counter from frame left, catching highlights on
the moving water surface; the rest of the room in deep shadow.

Look: cinematic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, cool desaturated palette, shallow focus f/2.8 on the
spilling edge of the tray.

Audio: glass shifting, water sloshing heavily, water hitting a tiled floor. Total room silence
otherwise. A woman whispers, barely audible: "Ay, no." No music.

NEGATIVE: no text, no watermark, no CGI water, no slow motion, no blue tinted water, no
splashing droplets in the air, no face visible, no camera movement.
```

# BLOQUE B — LA CUENTA

## T07 · El mapa de papel · 7 s

```
Overhead top-down shot, 35mm lens, static tripod.

A yellowed Argentine paper road map from 2003, spread flat over a car bonnet, repaired along
its folds with strips of yellowed adhesive tape. A thick, weathered, sun-spotted index finger
with a grease-blackened nail enters frame from the right.

The finger lands hard on a town in the east of the map and drags slowly west across the entire
sheet, following a road line, and stops at the mountain range. It taps the spot twice.

Setting: inside a small-town Argentine mechanic's workshop at dusk, the map on the bonnet of an
old Renault Torino, a single work lamp hanging directly above.

Light: one bare hanging work lamp, hot bright pool in the center of the map, rapid falloff to
darkness at the edges of frame.

Look: cinematic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, warm tungsten palette, macro texture of paper fibre,
folds and tape, shallow focus f/2.8.

Audio: paper crinkling, a fingertip tapping paper twice. An old man's voice, low and certain,
Argentine Spanish: "Ruta 3 hasta Bahía. Después la 22, todo derecho."

NEGATIVE: no text overlays, no watermark, no English place names, no modern branding, no GPS,
no phone, no camera movement, no zoom.
```

## T08 · El cajero sin billetes · 5 s

```
Extreme close-up, 85mm lens, static tripod.

An ATM screen, scratched and slightly glared, displaying in plain block Spanish text: "SIN
EXISTENCIA DE BILLETES". A man's hand enters frame and presses a rubber keypad button twice,
hard. The message does not change.

Setting: the exterior ATM of a small-town Argentine bank branch, mid-morning, the reflection of

Light: hard high mid-morning daylight, strong reflections on the glass, deep shadow in the ATM
alcove.

Look: cinematic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, slightly overexposed daylight, warm brick and concrete
palette, shallow focus f/2.8.

Audio: street ambience, a passing motorbike, two flat electronic error beeps. Several people
behind, nobody speaking. No music.

NEGATIVE: no bank logos, no brand names, no English text, no modern touchscreen interface,
no faces, no camera movement.
```

## T09 · La góndola vacía · 5 s

```
Slow lateral tracking shot, 40mm lens, dolly right at walking pace, steady.

The camera glides past three supermarket shelving units that are almost completely empty: bare
metal shelving, a few scattered tins on their sides, one broken bag of flour spilled white
across the floor tiles, an abandoned wire trolley standing crooked in the aisle.

Setting: a small-town Argentine grocery store at night, fluorescent ceiling tubes, worn
checkerboard floor tiles, hand-written cardboard price tags still clipped to the empty shelf
edges.

Light: overhead fluorescent tubes, flat greenish-white, one tube flickering, hard shadows under
the shelves.

Look: cinematic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, cold green-white fluorescent palette, deep focus f/5.6.

Audio: fluorescent hum, a flickering tube ticking, a trolley wheel squeaking somewhere off
frame, a metal roller shutter rattling far away. No voices. No music.

NEGATIVE: no text overlays, no watermark, no brand names, no readable product labels, no
people, no looting action, no broken glass, no smoke, no camera shake.
```

## T10 · La radio se queda muda · 5 s

```
Extreme close-up, 85mm macro lens, static tripod.

The front panel of an old VHF radio transceiver: an illuminated green frequency display, an
analogue signal-strength meter with a white needle, a chrome tuning knob, worn lettering.

The needle jumps twice, hard, to the right — and then falls flat to zero and stays there,
completely still, for the rest of the shot.

Setting: on the concrete floor of a dark workshop, a coil of cable and a car battery out of
focus beside it.

Light: only the green glow of the radio's own panel, lighting nothing else. Everything around
it is black.

Look: cinematic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, green monochrome palette, macro, extremely shallow
focus f/2.0.

Audio: a man's voice in Chilean Spanish cutting through heavy static, mid-sentence: "...están
evacuando el puerto, repito, están evacuando el..." — and then the voice stops mid-word and
there is only pure white noise for the last three seconds. No music.

NEGATIVE: no text overlays, no watermark, no brand names, no hands, no people, no modern
digital display, no camera movement.
```

## T11 · Humo detrás de la iglesia · 5 s

```
Wide static shot, 40mm lens, tripod, slightly low angle.

The silhouette of a small-town Argentine church tower against a night sky. Thick black smoke

Setting: a town square at night, plane trees in silhouette in the foreground, a monument base,

Light: orange fire glow from below and behind, blue-red police strobe sweeping across, one

Look: cinematic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, night palette of sodium orange against blue-black,
high contrast, smoke visible in the light beams, deep focus f/5.6.

Audio: a distant crowd, a siren, breaking glass, a car horn stuck on one note. No music.

NEGATIVE: no text, no watermark, no people in frame, no visible flames, no explosion, no
firefighters, no camera movement, no lens flare.
```

# BLOQUE C — LA SALIDA

## T12 · Las manos en la chapa · 6 s

```
Close-up, 50mm lens, handheld, travelling slowly alongside the moving truck.

Many human hands of different ages and sizes pressed against the white painted steel door of a
moving truck. The hands slap the metal, press flat, and slide along it as the truck creeps
forward. One hand grabs the door mirror arm and is pulled out of frame.

Setting: a small-town street at night, a dense crowd pressing in on both sides, headlights and
torch beams flashing across the white paintwork. A greasy handprint already on the door.

Light: hard mixed light — orange sodium streetlamp from above, white torch beams crossing,
strong moving shadows.

Look: cinematic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, night palette of sodium orange and blue-black, high
contrast, dust in the air, shallow focus f/2.8.

Audio: palms slapping steel, dozens of overlapping voices, a man shouting in Argentine Spanish,
desperate: "¡Llevame! ¡Llevame!" A diesel engine at low revs underneath. No music.

NEGATIVE: no text, no watermark, no faces in focus, no violence, no weapons, no blood, no
slow motion, no CGI crowd.
```

## T13 · El cartel de salida del pueblo · 6 s

```
Wide static shot, 28mm lens, tripod, from a low position at the roadside.

LA PORFIADA — a white 1996 Scania 113H long-haul truck, high cab, chrome bull bar, two vertical
exhaust stacks, sun-bleached paint, "LA PORFIADA" hand-painted in red script on the front
bumper, pulling a flatbed semi-trailer covered with a patched olive-green military tarpaulin
tied down with ropes.

The truck drives away from camera along a two-lane road, passing a roadside sign that reads
"TRES ARROYOS — GRACIAS POR SU VISITA". Its red tail lights shrink and shrink into total
darkness.

Setting: the edge of a small town at night. One last orange streetlamp, then flat open
farmland and complete blackness beyond.

Light: a single orange sodium streetlamp on the sign, then only the truck's red tail lights and
a faint band of stars.

Look: cinematic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, almost monochrome night palette, heavy grain in the
shadows, deep focus f/5.6.

Audio: the noise of a crowd cutting out sharply in one step. A heavy diesel stretching up
through three gears. Crickets returning. No music.

NEGATIVE: no text overlays, no watermark, no other vehicles, no people, no camera movement,
no lens flare, no modern truck design, no American truck.
```

## T14 · El espejo retrovisor · 7 s

```
Extreme close-up of a truck's wing mirror, 85mm lens, static, visible engine micro-vibration.

A dusty rectangular truck wing mirror in a black plastic housing. Inside the reflection: a
two-lane road receding into the night, a faint orange glow of a burning town very far away at
the end of it, and above the horizon line in the reflected sky — a single small point of cold
white light that is clearly not a star and not the moon, slightly elongated, too bright.

The whole reflection trembles with the engine. A human figure runs across the far end of the
reflected road and is gone.

Setting: mounted on the driver's door of a moving truck at night.

Light: orange sodium and distant fire glow inside the reflection, the mirror housing itself
almost black. The white point of light in the reflected sky is the brightest thing in frame.

Look: cinematic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, night palette, macro, extremely shallow focus f/2.0
on the mirror glass.

Audio: a heavy diesel at cruising revs, wind, and underneath it a very low sustained tone,
25 Hz, felt more than heard. No music until the last second.

NEGATIVE: no text, no watermark, no meteor trail, no fireball, no shooting star streak, no
explosion, no CGI object, no lens flare, no camera movement. The light in the sky must be a
small, still, ambiguous point — never an obvious meteor.
```
